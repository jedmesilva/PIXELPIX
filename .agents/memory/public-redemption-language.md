---
name: Public redemption language
description: User-facing redemption screens and emails must describe outcomes and follow-up without exposing internal administration or review processes.
---

Public redemption copy must never mention administration, internal systems, manual review, or operational approval steps. It should state that the request was received, show its public status, give the request date/time, and explain that updates arrive by email or through the certificate link.

**Why:** The redemption workflow is an internal operational process; exposing it creates unnecessary information for certificate holders and makes the product feel like an internal tool.

**How to apply:** Keep internal terms in admin-only surfaces and logs. Audit both the public page and every redemption email variant whenever statuses or payout wording change.
