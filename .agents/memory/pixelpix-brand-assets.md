---
name: PIXELPIX brand assets
description: The canonical relationship between the PIXELPIX inline logo and reusable public brand assets.
---

Use the six-block PIXELPIX mark already rendered by the app as the source of truth for favicons, manifests, social previews, and future logo exports. Keep separate symbol-only and symbol-plus-wordmark assets.

**Why:** An earlier favicon approximation omitted one block and made the browser icon visibly different from the product logo.

**How to apply:** When creating or changing brand assets, copy the exact six rectangle positions and proportions from the existing inline mark; do not redraw a simplified five-block version.
